Open your notes, and the text floats beside your mirrored webcam image. You edit with your hands: select words, turn a dial to change a sentence's tone, or move two hands apart to expand a paragraph.

Then you rehearse out loud. PalmCards follows your voice through the notes and measures pace, fillers, pauses and where you looked, so you can compare each take with the last. It all runs on your Mac and works offline.
