Open a `.txt`, `.md` or `.docx` file, and its text floats beside your mirrored webcam image. You edit with your hands: select words, sentences or paragraphs, explore alternative words, turn a dial to change a sentence's tone, or move two hands apart to expand a paragraph.

Then you rehearse out loud. PalmCards follows your voice through the notes and measures each take: pace, fillers, pauses, pitch range, where you looked, and posture. It sets your takes side by side, so you can compare this one with the last. It observes; it does not grade.

Everything runs on your Mac, and it works offline with a local language model through Ollama. The cloud model is optional.
